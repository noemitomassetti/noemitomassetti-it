The goal is not to imitate wording, borrow client names or claim competitor results. No unverified competitor claims should be reused.

## Positioning: LinkedIn is the source of truth
Multilingual virtual assistance, organisational secretarial work, operational back office, existing-customer care, professional communications, digital content, and **professional translation in both directions between Italian and English/French/Spanish**.

Excluded from public services: business administration, bookkeeping, invoicing, telemarketing, cold outreach, lead generation, commercial contact sourcing, recruiting interviewees, and result-based sales appointments.

## Implemented in the review branch
- Portfolio metadata in four languages corrected to IT ↔ EN/FR/ES.
- Three original, fictional and fully written reverse-direction translation dossiers added: IT→EN customer order update; IT→FR training communication; IT→ES product information.
- These include complete source and target passages, terminology choices, method, review notes and deliverable summaries in four interface languages.
- The deep portfolio now has **9 translation dossiers** (6 existing EN/FR/ES→IT + 3 new IT→EN/FR/ES) and **15 operational dossiers**, **24 total**, alongside the existing 18 short cases and 22 document examples.
- All demonstration cases must remain prominently labelled as fictional, never commissioned client work.

## Quality gaps before release
1. Audit all route-specific copy, service descriptions, forms, CTAs, metadata, blog and portfolio in IT/EN/FR/ES for consistency, idiomatic style, and exclusions.
2. Review every source/target translation pair independently for fidelity, terminology, register and cultural adaptation. Automated checks are not human linguistic certification.
3. Enrich operational cases with full usable artefacts (before/after inbox handling, order register, price-list change log, customer-response templates, content revisions, data validation checklists), all with synthetic data.
4. Ensure the visitor can filter by service, language direction, industry and type of deliverable; link cases to a single written quote-request CTA.
5. Check every newly added dossier route, responsive layout, keyboard interaction, contrast and SEO/hreflang on the actual preview.
6. Run CI/build and browser checks on the current commit; repeat the real Web3Forms email delivery test on the exact release candidate.
7. Do not merge to main or publish to production until checks and user approval.

## Important editorial distinction
A specialised page about *Italian-market localisation* may legitimately show examples and prices for translation **into Italian**. However it must never imply that all professional translation services are limited to that direction.
